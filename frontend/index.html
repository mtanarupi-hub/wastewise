Why WasteWise?

One scan, full answer
Point your camera at any household item and instantly get disposal rules, drop-off locations, and hours.
Built for Middlesex County
All data comes directly from the MCIA booklet. Rules for tires, paint, HHW events — all in one place.
Protecting our waterways
Hazardous materials disposed incorrectly contaminate local waterways. WasteWise helps residents dispose safely.
Your personal inventory
Track what you need to dispose of, set reminders, and check items off as you drop them off.

How to use

  1. 1
    Scan — take a photo of any item you are unsure about.
  2. 2
    Review — see disposal rules and nearby drop-off sites.
  3. 3
    Save — add it to your inventory and set a reminder.
  4. 4
    Dispose — get directions and check it off when done.
